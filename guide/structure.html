01 / The Concept

When scale breaks perception.

We exist in a universe defined by scale, from the subatomic to the cosmic. But what happens when our intuitive understanding of size, impact, and time fails us?

Disorders of Magnitude explores the spaces where traditional metrics break down. It is an examination of exponential growth, cascading failures, microscopic shifts that trigger macroscopic avalanches, and the profound realization that everything we know is just a matter of perspective.

Join us as visionaries across science, art, technology, and sociology attempt to map the unmappable and measure the immeasurable.

Macro Global systems, societal shifts, planetary impact.
Micro Quantum mechanics, individual actions, cellular change.
